Everything else

Reminders that arrive

A week, a day, an hour or ten minutes before, whichever you pick. They are sent from the server, so they reach you with the app closed, and for a booking someone else in your apartment made.

A countdown on the Lock Screen

In the hour before your slot a Live Activity ticks down in the Dynamic Island, then keeps going through the fifteen minutes you have to tag in before the booking is released.

A list worth scrolling

Hide the hours you would never book and the groups in the other building. Jump to a date instead of paging a week at a time, or long press a time to book it without opening anything.

The rules for your room

Max future bookings, max per week and month, and how long your tag still opens the door after a session. Transcribed from sssb.se and shown for your street address.

Straight into your calendar

Add a booking as a calendar event with an alert at the start, if a reminder on the Lock Screen is not enough.

Swedish when your phone is

The whole app follows the language your phone is set to, with SSSB's own words for the object number, the groups and the laundry room.
